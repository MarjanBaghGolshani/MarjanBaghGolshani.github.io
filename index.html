Hi, I'm Marjan.

I make AI systems safer, and use them to understand cells and cancer.

I'm a PhD candidate in the School of Computing at Macquarie University and a Research Assistant at the Centre for Applied Artificial Intelligence. My PhD studies how LLM agents can collude, and how to detect it from inside the models. Alongside this, I work with Macquarie Medical School on LLM-based tools for personalised cancer vaccine design.
