About & CV

I trained as a software engineer, moved into bioinformatics, and now work where machine learning, biology and AI safety meet. My work so far has covered generating drug molecules with reinforcement learning, predicting peptide–HLA binding for cancer vaccines, and building interpretable models of gene regulation. In my PhD I'm turning to the safety of the AI systems themselves.
